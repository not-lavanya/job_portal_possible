# Product Goals - Checkable Metrics (2026-10-22)

1. **Application completion time** — Actual time to complete vs recruiter's stated estimate
2. **Application volume** — Average applications per student per year (sophomore/junior)
3. **Time investment vs outcome** — Hours spent applying vs offers/rejections
4. **Rejection rate delta** — Does the solution empirically reduce rejection rates?
5. **Centralization trust** — Why don't companies trust centralized systems vs building own ATS?
6. **Error/discard rate** — How many applications discarded due to wrong/missing information
