## Problem Area Selected

Work & Internships (job search, daily workplace friction, side gigs)

## Pain Points Identified

### Inefficient
- Notification/status of the stage of my job application alongside how many applicants have applied for the same role

### Frustrating & Inefficient
- New forms for each job application, which makes it frustrating and time-consuming

### Confusing & Inefficient
- No info cards & time needed for what that form box means or wants from us from recruiter perspective

### Confusing
- Software requirements are not physically possible or vague. E.g. a software requirement with 5 years of experience, but it came out 3 years back

### Frustrating
- Reason for rejection

## Drafted Problem Statement

College students struggle to complete job and internship applications efficiently and accurately because the major application platforms are built around the employer\'s hiring workflow, not the applicant\'s experience. Each employer runs its own form, account, and wording, and none of the existing platforms fully solves reuse, clarity, or feedback. Applicants retype the same information, guess at vague instructions and rarely learn their status or why they were rejected, which leads to wasted time, avoidable rejections, uncertainty, and frustration.

## Jobs to Be Done

1. Applying without starting over
2. Understanding what\'s being asked
3. Knowing where I stand

## Objective

Achieve clearer communication between people seeking jobs/internships and employers during the application process, so applicants spend less time on each application, don\'t have to retype the same information for every employer, and face fewer rejections caused by vague or incorrect requirements.

## Team Reflection (2026-10-22)

The fiction is in the process, the structural form of how these platforms are designed.
We saw that filling those different forms continuously is a very time-consuming, repetitive task, which can be automated because every single company has its own ATS.
We, as students, cannot leave the application in the middle, but at times it can get so time-consuming. Since this is not a part of our job, it is time-consuming to continue filling those applications and be correct.

## Interview Questions

1. Tell me how much time it usually takes you to finish a job application.
2. How often do you find yourself stuck in the middle of a job application because the questions are too ambiguous?
3. How many times have you felt it took you longer to complete your job profile than needed?
4. Do you feel frustrated filling out new job applications from scratch for each company you\'re applying to?
5. After submitting your application, what updates did you receive? How long did you wait for updating in average?
6. How often do you have to re-enter information that you\'ve already provided during the applications?
