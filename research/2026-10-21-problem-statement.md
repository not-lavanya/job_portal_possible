# Problem Statement

College students struggle to complete job and internship applications efficiently and accurately because the major application platforms are built around the employer\'s hiring workflow, not the applicant\'s experience. Each employer runs its own form, account, and wording, and none of the existing platforms fully solves reuse, clarity, or feedback. Applicants retype the same information, guess at vague instructions and rarely learn their status or why they were rejected, which leads to wasted time, avoidable rejections, uncertainty, and frustration.

# Target User

College students applying to jobs and internships

# What We Learned from Research

Key findings from Monday\'s session about job application pain points:
- Separate accounts and forms per employer
- Resume parsing that does not remove retyping
- Reuse tools that stop at employer\'s form
- Vague fields with no explanation
- No sense of how long an application will take
- Unrealistic or unclear requirements
- Almost no visibility into status or competition
- Rejections with no reason

# Product Goals

- Achieve clearer communication between people seeking jobs/internships and employers during the application process
- Reduce time spent on each application
- Eliminate retyping of same information for every employer
- Reduce rejections caused by vague or incorrect requirements
- Provide better visibility into application status
- Give clear reasons for rejections

# Solutions We Considered

1. Centralized applicant profile that syncs across all platforms
2. Enhanced application with context and examples for each field
3. Status tracking and communication dashboard
4. Smart rejection analysis and feedback system
5. Application time estimator
